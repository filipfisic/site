Business Consulting

Starting a business or already running one, but you have a pile of questions no one gives clear answers to? Do you need a trade (obrt) or something else? How do you write a business plan? Is the idea even worth it? We sort it out simply, concretely and without the paperwork that scares you. What takes you weeks is our everyday work.

What we help you with:

Business Plan

We write a business plan for the bank, an investor or for yourself, with a realistic calculation of revenue, costs and the profitability of your idea.

Self-Employment Plan (HZZ grant)

We prepare a complete business plan for the Croatian Employment Service self-employment measure, ready for submission and with everything needed to obtain the grant.

Trade, Ltd. or Something Else?

We help you choose the right business form (sole trade, flat-rate trade, OPG, j.d.o.o., d.o.o., d.d…) based on your situation, so you don't pay more than you must.

Starting a Business Step by Step

We guide you through the entire registration process: which documents, in what order, which activity codes and what to watch out for so you start without mistakes.

Finding Grants and Subsidies

We track and check which grants and non-repayable funds you qualify for and help with the application.

Order in an Existing Business – Reorganization

We look at where your money and time are unnecessarily leaking and propose concrete changes that pay off.

Advice When Deciding on Expansion

A second pair of eyes and experience when you're considering growth, hiring staff or a major change, so you can be sure before you spend money.

HR Documentation and Work Processes

We take over the paperwork around employees and help organize work processes and documentation, HR records and the structuring of employment relationships – properly and on time. When a situation with employees arises, we're by your side to handle it before it grows into a problem.

Complete Support for Your Business

Business challenges rarely belong to just one area. A business decision often involves organizational, financial, administrative, tax or legal questions at the same time, which is why it is important to look at your business as a whole.

At Providentia, we help you connect all of these elements and turn them into clear, concrete and actionable steps.

We provide business, organizational and administrative consulting, and when a particular project or business situation calls for legal expertise, we work with attorneys whom we bring into the process according to the needs of the specific client and project.

This way, the client does not have to search for solutions and coordinate multiple experts on their own. Providentia leads and coordinates the business side of the process, while legal matters are handled by the attorneys we work with – each within their own expertise and authority.

One Business Goal. Connected Expertise.

From starting a business and developing new projects to reorganization, hiring, expansion or other important business decisions, our goal is to look at the whole picture, recognize what is needed and help you organize the path to a solution.

When additional expertise is needed to make it happen, we do not leave you to figure out whom to turn to on your own.

Through our network of professional associates we can, depending on the needs of the project, bring in attorneys and other appropriately licensed experts. That way the business, administrative and professional aspects of a project can be coordinated with one another, and you get a clearer, simpler process from the initial idea to realization.

Business Support Tailored to Real Needs

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Every business, project and entrepreneur has their own circumstances, goals and challenges.

That is why we first understand your situation, then define what needs to be done and, when necessary, bring together the right experts so that you have connected support from all relevant areas on a single project.

Our role is to be your partner in the business process – connecting the people, information and steps you need to make decisions in an organized way, with a clear picture of what comes next.

You focus on your business. We help connect everything it needs to function.

Providentia provides business, organizational and administrative consulting services. Legal aid and attorney services are not provided by Providentia; when needed, they are provided by the attorneys involved in the project, in accordance with their professional authorizations.
