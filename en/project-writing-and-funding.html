Project Writing & Funding

There are non-repayable funds for your idea – for equipment, business expansion, hiring or a new project. The problem is that calls are full of rules, deadlines and documentation in which it's easy to get lost. We take that part on: from checking whether you can even apply, through writing the application, to reporting after you receive the funds. You focus on your business, we handle the paperwork.

What we help you with:

Eligibility Check

First we check whether you meet the conditions and whether it even makes sense to apply, so you don't waste time on a call that isn't for you.

Finding the Right Call

We monitor open calls (EU funds, ministries, counties, cities) and find the one that fits your idea and activity.

Writing the Project Application

We write the complete application and project documentation, with a clearly developed idea, objectives and a budget that passes evaluation.

Submitting the Application

We prepare and submit the application with all attachments, on time and according to the call's rules.

Project Implementation

Once you receive the funds, we run the project through the entire implementation: tracking activities, deadlines and costs per the contract.

Reporting and Payment Requests

We prepare reports and payment requests and communicate with the relevant institutions, so the money arrives on time and without problems.

Final Report and Records

We compile the final documentation and records, so the project is properly closed and ready for any inspection.

We can run the entire process on a „turnkey” basis – from the first idea to the last report – or help you with just the part you need. Have an idea but don't know if there's funding for it? Get in touch, we'll check and tell you honestly what your chances are.
